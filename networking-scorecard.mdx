The **Network Intelligence Score (NIS)** is the score card that appears at the top of your MyEventJar **Home** screen once your profile is verified. It turns your day-to-day networking — contacts added, events attended, follow-ups sent, meetings held, referrals made, and revenue logged — into a single number you can grow over time, measured against a **target you set for yourself** each month.

## Before Your NIS Is Calculated

MyEventJar keeps the score card locked until you complete three prerequisites, so your NIS reflects real activity from a verified profile.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Verify Your Email" icon="envelope-circle-check">
    Confirm your email address from the link sent to your inbox.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Verify Your Phone" icon="mobile-screen">
    Confirm your phone number with the one-time code sent to your WhatsApp.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Add At Least One Contact" icon="user-plus">
    Add your first contact via NFC, QR code, event, manual entry, or card scan.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Once all three are complete, your NIS card unlocks on **Home** and starts scoring your activity immediately.

## The NIS Card on Home

Once unlocked, the top of your Home screen shows:

- Your **total contact count**
- Your current **NIS score**, shown as an **RPM-style gauge** — your **current points**, the sum of all six category scores out of a maximum of **100**, plotted against your **target points** for the current **target month**, the same way a speedometer needle sits against its dial

Tap the card to open the full **NIS Dashboard**.

## Network Milestones

The Dashboard shows a progress bar marking where you sit across four milestone tiers, based on your total contact count:

| Tier | Contacts |
| --- | --- |
| **Starter** | Up to 50 |
| **Connector** | 50 – 250 |
| **Networker** | 250 – 500 |
| **Champion** | 500+ (1,000+ marks an especially strong network) |

## The NIS Dashboard

Tapping the Home card opens the Dashboard, which repeats your current score and target, and adds:

- **Monthly Trend** — a horizontally scrollable card showing how your score has moved over recent months, alongside the six category cards below.
- **Quote of the Day** — a short networking or business quote, refreshed daily around 9:00 PM.
- **Quick Actions** — two cards just below the quote:
  - **Overdue Follow-up** — jumps you to contacts whose follow-up is overdue.
  - **Reconnect** — surfaces a quality contact you haven't engaged with in a while.
  Tap **Take Action** on either card to go straight to the right screen — completing the action raises your NIS score.

## The NIS Breakdown

Each of the six categories appears as a tappable row on the Dashboard, adding up to a maximum of **100 points**:

- **Network Worth** — 20 pts
- **Follow-up Effectiveness** — 25 pts
- **Relationship Strength** — 20 pts
- **Connection Activity** — 15 pts
- **Opportunity Generation** — 10 pts
- **Revenue Impact** — 10 pts

Tap any category to open a **bottom drawer** showing exactly what raises its points. See Your Score for what each category tracks and exactly what to do to raise it.
