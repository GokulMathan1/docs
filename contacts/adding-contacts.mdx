---
title: "Adding Contacts to Your MyEventJar Network: All Methods"
sidebarTitle: "Adding Contacts"
description: "Add contacts in MyEventJar via physical visiting card scan, NFC card tap, manual entry, QR code scan, or from event attendees."
---

Growing your network in MyEventJar is designed to be fast and flexible, no matter the situation. You might scan a paper visiting card at your desk, tap an NFC-enabled MyEventJar card at an event, add someone manually, scan another user's QR code across the room, or add someone from the attendee list of an event you are at. MyEventJar supports five ways to add contacts.

When you meet someone at a [Business Event](/business-events/overview), they're first added as a **Connection** rather than a full contact — see the **Event Attendees** tab below for how a connection gets converted into a contact.

<Note>
  **Bulk import is web-only.** To import many contacts at once (CSV upload or phone contacts import), sign in at [myeventjar.com](https://myeventjar.com) and go to **Networking → Import**. The mobile app does not support bulk import.
</Note>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Scan Physical Visiting Card">
    Turn any paper business card into a MyEventJar contact in seconds. MyEventJar's card scanner reads the name, company, title, phone, email, and website directly from a photo.

    **To scan a physical visiting card:**

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the card scanner">
        Tap the **\+** button in the Contacts tab and select **Scan Visiting Card**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Capture the card">
        Take a new photo of the card on a flat, well-lit surface, or choose an existing photo from your **gallery**. Line it up inside the on-screen frame and MyEventJar captures it automatically. Flip the card over and tap **Scan Back** if there are extra details on the reverse.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Review the extracted details">
        MyEventJar reads the card and fills in each field. Review the extracted name, company, title, phone, email, and website, and correct anything the scanner misread.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add a note and save">
        Add a short note about where you met, then tap **Save Contact**. The original card photo is attached to the contact so you can reference it later.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="NFC Card Tap">
    If you or the person you meet has a MyEventJar NFC business card, adding each other to contacts is as simple as tapping the card to a phone — no app open, no scanning required.

    **To add a contact via NFC:**

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Unlock your phone">
        NFC only fires on unlocked devices. Wake your phone and unlock it — you do not need to open MyEventJar first.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Tap the NFC card to your phone">
        Hold the MyEventJar NFC card against the back of your phone (near the top on most devices). Your phone vibrates when the tap is detected.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open the MyEventJar prompt">
        A notification appears with the contact's name and photo. Tap it to open the contact preview in MyEventJar.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add a note and save">
        Optionally record where you met, then tap **Add to Contacts**. The contact's full profile is imported instantly.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Tip>
      Order your own MyEventJar NFC card from **Profile → My Digital Business Card → Order NFC Card**. When someone taps it, they get your live profile even if they do not have MyEventJar installed.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Manual Entry">
    Manual entry gives you full control over the details you save, making it the best choice when you have a business card in hand, are transcribing from an email signature, or want to add a personal contact with a note.

    **To add a contact manually:**

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the Add Contact screen">
        Tap the **\+** button in the top-right corner of the Contacts tab, then select **Add Manually**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter the required details">
        Fill in the contact's **Name**, **Email**, and **Mobile Number** — these are the only required fields, everything else is optional. New contacts are automatically placed in the **New** stage.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Write an introductory note">
        Use the **Notes** field to capture context while it's fresh — where you met, what you discussed, or a personal detail worth remembering.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Save the contact">
        Tap **Save Contact**. The contact is added to your list immediately and is searchable right away.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Tip>
      Use the **Duplicate Check** feature — MyEventJar will warn you if a contact with the same mobile number or email already exists, helping you avoid clutter before you save.
    </Tip>

    <Note>
      If the person you're adding is already a MyEventJar user, adding them also adds you as a connection on their side — it's a two-way action whenever both people are on MyEventJar.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Scan a QR Code">
    Every MyEventJar user has a personal Digital Business Card with a unique QR code. When you meet someone who also uses MyEventJar, scanning their code instantly imports their full, up-to-date contact details — no typing required.

    **To scan a QR code:**

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the Scanner">
        Tap the **\+** button in the Contacts tab and select **Scan QR Code**, or tap the QR icon on the home screen's quick-action bar.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Point your camera at their code">
        Hold your camera steady over the other person's Digital Business Card QR code — on their phone screen, a printed card, or a badge. MyEventJar detects the code automatically without needing to press a shutter button.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Review the imported details">
        A preview card appears showing the contact's name, company, role, and contact details exactly as they have them set in their MyEventJar profile.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add a personal note and save">
        Optionally jot a quick note about where you met or what you talked about, then tap **Add to Contacts** to save.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Tip>
      At events and conferences, set your own QR code to display on your lock screen so others can scan you just as easily. Go to **Profile → My Digital Business Card → Show QR Code** and tap **Add to Lock Screen**.
    </Tip>

    <Note>
      When a contact you added via QR code updates their MyEventJar profile (new job, new number), you'll receive a notification and can accept the update with one tap — keeping their record current automatically.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Event Attendees">
    When you attend a business event in MyEventJar, every other attendee becomes reachable from that event's page. Rather than landing straight in your Contacts, attending together first adds someone as a **Connection** — a lighter-weight link you can convert into a full contact whenever you're ready.

    **To add a contact from an event:**

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the event">
        Go to the **Events** tab and open an event you are attending or have attended. Tap the **Attendees** section.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Find the attendee">
        Scroll or search the attendee list. Each attendee shows their name, company, and role. Tap a name to see their full profile as they have chosen to share it at the event.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Connect with the attendee">
        Tap **Connect** _(exact label to confirm)_ on their profile to add them as a **Connection**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Convert the connection to a contact">
        From your **Connections** list, open the attendee and choose **Add to Contacts** to promote them from a connection to a full contact. Optionally add a note about your conversation, a session you both attended, or a follow-up idea, then tap **Save**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Tip>
      Attendee visibility is controlled per event. If you cannot see someone in the attendee list, they have opted out of attendee discovery for that event.
    </Tip>

    <Note>
      Converting a connection into a contact starts the 24-hour, 7-day, and 30-day follow-up cadence automatically. See [Follow-ups & Insights](/contacts/follow-ups-insights) to work them into a routine.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
