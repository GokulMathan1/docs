The Contacts section in MyEventJar is your central hub for every person in your professional and personal network. Whether you're catching up with a colleague you met at a conference or staying on top of a growing client list, the Contacts section gives you the tools to keep everything tidy, searchable, and up to date.

## Searching Your Contact List

Use the **Search** bar at the top of the Contacts screen to find anyone in your network instantly. MyEventJar searches across names, companies, roles, email addresses, and custom tags as you type.

There's no separate filter option — instead, your list keeps itself organized automatically. It's sorted by **most recently updated** by default: whenever a contact gets an update — a new meeting logged, a note added, or any field edited — that contact automatically moves to the top of the list, so your most active relationships are always easiest to find.

## Organizing Contacts with Tags

Tags let you segment your network so you can find the right people at the right time — stack as many flexible tags as you like on any contact.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a Contact">
    Tap any contact's name or photo to open their detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap 'Edit'">
    Select the **Edit** button in the top-right corner of the contact detail screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Tags">
    In the **Tags** field, type a tag name and press **Enter** or tap the suggested tag to apply it. Add as many tags as you need — for example, `#followup`, `#NYC`, `#design`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Changes">
    Tap **Save** to apply the tags. The contact immediately matches any tag-based searches.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can also bulk-assign tags. In the contact list, long-press any contact to enter selection mode, tap additional contacts to select them, then choose **Organize** from the action bar at the bottom of the screen to apply a tag to everyone selected at once.
</Note>

## Syncing with EventJar Users

If a contact is also a MyEventJar user, MyEventJar matches them by their **email address** and automatically keeps their **name**, **email**, and **mobile number** in sync with what they've set on their own profile — so their details stay accurate without any work on your part.

<Note>
  Because these fields are controlled by the contact's own EventJar profile, editing their name or mobile number yourself won't be reflected — your changes won't stick as long as they stay matched by email. This sync stays active through the follow-up cadence, but it stops at lead qualification — qualifying a lead is a manual, one-way step on each side.
</Note>

## Follow-up Cadence & Overdue Status

Every new contact enters a follow-up cadence with three checkpoints: **24 hours**, **7 days**, and **30 days** after you add them. Miss a checkpoint's window and that contact is marked **Overdue** for it.

## Deleting Contacts

Deleting a contact **permanently removes** them along with their notes and follow-up progress. This action cannot be undone.

To delete a contact, swipe left on their entry in the contact list and tap **Delete**, or open the contact detail page, tap the **⋯ More** menu, and select **Delete**.
