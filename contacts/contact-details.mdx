Every contact in MyEventJar has a detail page that goes beyond just a name and phone number. Alongside the basics, you can add tags, notes, and revenue — giving you useful context on each person in your network every time you open their profile.

## Available Contact Fields

Every contact record in MyEventJar keeps the same simple structure:

| Field | Required? | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Name** | Required | The contact's full name. |
| **Email** | Required | Their email address. |
| **Phone Number** | Required | Their primary phone number. |
| **Stage** | Auto-set | Defaults to **New** when the contact is added, then moves through the [follow-up cadence](/contacts/follow-ups) as you complete each stage. |
| **Tags** | Optional | Flexible, customizable labels for filtering and search. |
| **Notes** | Optional | Free-text notes about the contact or relationship. |

### Additional Info

Tap **Additional Info** on a contact's card to expand a few more optional fields:

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Phone 2** | A second phone number. |
| **Company** | The organization the contact works for. |
| **Website** | Personal or company website URL. |
| **Address** | Mailing or office address. |

## Editing a Contact

You can update any field on a contact's detail page at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contact">
    Tap the contact's name in your contact list to open their detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap 'Edit'">
    Tap the **Edit** button in the top-right corner of the screen. All fields become editable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Tap any field to update it, including **Phone 2**, **Company**, **Website**, or **Address** under **Additional Info**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Tap **Save**. Your changes are saved instantly and reflected everywhere this contact appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If a contact is also a MyEventJar user, their **name** and **mobile number** on your saved card will always match their own MyEventJar account. Editing those two fields yourself won't stick, since they stay synced from the contact's own profile rather than from your edits.
</Note>

## Adding Notes

MyEventJar doesn't keep a separate interaction timeline — instead, jot context straight into the contact's **Notes** field. Open the contact, tap **Edit**, update **Notes** with what you discussed, a helpful introduction they made, or anything else worth remembering, then tap **Save**.

<Tip>
  After every event or meeting, spend 60 seconds updating a new contact's Notes while the details are fresh. This small habit makes MyEventJar dramatically more valuable when you need to reconnect weeks or months later.
</Tip>

## Mark Revenue

If a contact leads to real business — a client signs on, a deal closes, a sale happens — log it as **Revenue** directly on their profile. Tracking revenue against a contact helps you see how your networking is contributing toward your monthly target.

Open the contact's detail page and tap **Mark Revenue** to log an amount against that contact. See [Networking Intelligence Score](/networking-scorecard) to learn how revenue and your other networking activity roll up into your NIS.
