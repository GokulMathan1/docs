MyEventJar uses the same set of methods for both **Login** and **Create Account** — with one exception: **Phone + WhatsApp OTP** can only be used to log in to an account that already exists, not to create a new one. Choose whichever is easiest for you when signing up.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Email & Password" icon="envelope">
    Sign up with a valid email address and a password of your choice.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Phone + WhatsApp OTP" icon="whatsapp" iconType="brands">
    Login only. Once your phone number is verified on your profile, log in anytime by entering it and confirming the one-time code sent to your WhatsApp.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Social Login" icon="right-to-bracket">
    Continue with **Google**, **LinkedIn**, or **Apple** — no separate password needed.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Create Your Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the MyEventJar app">
    Install MyEventJar from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/myeventjar/id6755136085)(iOS) or [**Google Play Store**](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.myeventjar.eventjar_app&hl=en_IN) (Android), or open the app on the device where it is already installed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your sign-up method">
    On the welcome screen, tap **Create Account** and pick one:

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Email & Password">
        Enter your **email address**, create a **password**, then tap **Continue**. You will also be asked to add your **phone number** so you can verify it later — this is required to unlock your Network Intelligence Score (NIS).
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Google, LinkedIn, or Apple">
        Tap **Continue with Google**, **Continue with LinkedIn**, or **Continue with Apple**. Approve the sign-in prompt from that provider. Your name, email, and profile photo are imported automatically — you can edit them at any time. You'll also be asked to add your **phone number** on the next screen; unlike with email sign-up, this is **mandatory** for social sign-ups, not optional.

        <Warning>
          We don't recommend signing up with **Apple** where it can be avoided. If you do use **Continue with Apple**, choose **Share My Email** rather than **Hide My Email** when prompted. Meeting invites can't be delivered to Apple's private relay (proxy) address, so signing up with a hidden email will cause you to miss meeting invites and other interactions.
        </Warning>
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    If you signed up with email, open the verification email and tap **Verify My Email**. If you signed up with social, add your email under **Profile → Account** and verify it there.

    <Note>
      Verifying your email is required to unlock the **Network Intelligence Score (NIS)** dashboard.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your phone number">
    Your phone number is collected during sign-up either way, so it's already on your profile. Go to **Profile → Account → Phone**, tap **Send OTP**, and enter the code delivered on **WhatsApp** to verify it.

    <Note>
      Verifying your phone number is also required to unlock the **Network Intelligence Score (NIS)** dashboard.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your profile">
    Add your **full name**, **professional title**, **company**, **industry**, **location**, **profile photo**, and a short **bio**. This information appears on your **Digital Business Card** and Bio Page, so make it count.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add at least one contact">
    Before your NIS is calculated, add at least one contact — via **NFC card tap**, **QR code scan**, **event attendee**, **visiting card scan**, or **manual entry**. See [Add Contacts](/contacts/adding-contacts) for every method.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Land on Home">
    Tap **Let's Go!** to arrive at your home dashboard. Your **NIS score card** is at the top, ready to update as you network. See [Network Intelligence Score](/networking-scorecard/overview) to learn how the score works.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You Need Before NIS Unlocks

MyEventJar keeps the score card locked until you have proven you are a real, active networker. To unlock it:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Verify Email" icon="envelope-circle-check">
    Confirm your email address from the verification link or code.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Verify Phone" icon="mobile-screen">
    Confirm your phone number with the WhatsApp OTP.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Add 1 Contact" icon="user-plus">
    Add at least one contact using any supported method.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
