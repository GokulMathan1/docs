MyEventJar is designed to keep everything you need within easy reach. Once you know where to look, you can jump from your dashboard to your contacts, schedule a meeting, or find an event in just a tap or two. This guide walks you through every major section of the app so you can move around with confidence from day one.

## Main Navigation Tabs

The five tabs in the navigation bar are your primary way to move around the app. Tap any tab to go directly to that section.

<Columns cols={2}>

  * 👥  **Network** — Your full contact list with search, filters, tags, and individual contact profiles.

  * 👤  **Profile** — Your public profile, account settings, preferences, and notifications.

  * 📅  **Meetings** — All scheduled, pending, and past meetings; calendar view; meeting notes.

  * ▦  **More** — Secondary tools without a permanent spot on the bar: Events, Expense Split, all shortcuts, and more.

## Home / Dashboard

The **Home** tab is your command centre. Every time you open MyEventJar, this is where you land. The dashboard is divided into digestible sections:

- **Network Intelligence Score** — **NIS** is the score card that appears at the top of your MyEventJar home screen.
- **Recommended Events** — Events curated for you based on your interests, location, and networking goals.

Tap the NIS card on the dashboard to jump straight to the NIS Dashboard section, and tap Events to navigate away from the home screen.

## Network

The **Network** tab is your central address book — you'll need to be signed in to access your contacts. Here's what you can do:

- Follow every new contact through a **24-hour, 7-day, and 30-day** cadence, then qualify them as a lead — all from the Contacts tab in MyEventJar.

- **Overdue, Connections,** and **Meetings** also live under the Network tab.

## Profile & Settings

The **Profile** tab is both your public-facing page and your app control panel:

- **View and edit your profile** — Tap **Edit Profile** to update your photo, bio, title, contact info, or social links at any time.
- **Account & Security** — Change your password, manage connected social accounts, or turn on **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)** for extra login security. Your email address can't be changed once it's set.
- **Notifications** — There's no granular alert customization yet; this screen manages device **permissions** for **camera** and **notification** access.
- **Networking Preferences** — Update your goals, event interests, and availability
- **Integrations** — Connect **SMTP** for email delivery, **WhatsApp** via [MyBotify](https://whatsapp.mybotify.com/), and your **Google Calendar , Zoom Connect**

## Meetings

The **Meetings** tab keeps all your scheduled time in one place:

- **Upcoming meetings** are shown first; tap any meeting to see its details, attendees, agenda, and attached notes.
- **Schedule a new meeting** by tapping the **\+** button. You can invite MyEventJar contacts directly, set a location (physical or virtual link), add an agenda, and choose reminder timing.
- **Meeting notes** — After a meeting wraps up, tap **Add Notes** on its detail page to log key takeaways, action items, or follow-up reminders.
- **Past meetings** are archived automatically and remain searchable in the **History** sub-tab.

## More

The **More** tab (the 2×2 grid icon) is where you'll find the tools that don't have a permanent spot on the main bar, including **Events**, **Expense Split**, etc.
