MyEventJar brings your entire networking life into one place. Whether you're an **independent professional** juggling client relationships, a professional growing your industry connections, or someone who simply wants to stay on top of the people and events that matter most, MyEventJar gives you the tools to do it effortlessly — from a single, beautifully organized app.

## Who Is It For?

MyEventJar is designed for anyone who values their relationships and their time:

- **Professionals and executives** who manage a large network of colleagues, clients, and partners
- **Entrepreneurs and independent professionals** who need to stay on top of meetings, leads, and follow-ups

- **Event organizers and attendees** who collaborate through one-on-one meetings at organized events and want a single hub for their social and professional calendar
- **Anyone** who wants to remember names, faces, and the context behind every connection

## How MyEventJar Works

At its core, MyEventJar follows a simple loop that keeps your network active and growing:

1. **Connect** — Add contacts by scanning a business card, tapping NFC, scanning a QR code, entering details manually, or meeting people at events.
2. **Engage** — Schedule meetings, exchange messages, and log interaction notes.
3. **Grow** — Discover new events, expand your network, and track your relationship momentum over time.

Every feature in the app is designed to support this cycle, so nurturing your network becomes a natural part of your day rather than a chore.
