## Book a Ticket

Open an event's detail page and tap **Book Ticket**. For paid events, you'll go through checkout; for free events, your ticket is confirmed immediately.

## My Tickets

Find every ticket you've booked under **More → Events & Tickets → My Tickets**, split into **Upcoming** and **Completed**. Each ticket has a **QR code** — show it at the event for entry.

## Connect with Attendees

Once you've booked, open the event's **Attendees** list to see who else is going. If the organizer has enabled attendee connections for this event, you can add fellow attendees directly as contacts. See [Adding Contacts](/contacts/adding-contacts) for how that works, including how it starts the same follow-up cadence as any other new contact.

## Follow Up After the Event

Anyone you add as a contact at an event automatically starts the normal follow-up cadence. See [Follow-ups & Insights](/contacts/follow-ups-insights) for the 24-hour, 7-day, and 30-day steps.
