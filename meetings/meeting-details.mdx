The Meeting Details screen is your single source of truth for everything related to a specific meeting. From here you can review who's attending, track the meeting's current status, take notes, and take direct action — all without leaving the app. Tap any meeting in your **Meetings** list to open its detail view.

## What's on the Meeting Details screen

The detail screen is organized into clearly labeled sections:

- **Participants** — Shows the profile photo, name, and role (Organizer or Invitee) of everyone in the meeting. Tap a participant's name to open their **Digital Business Card**, or jump to their saved contact record from there.
- **Date, Time & Location** — Displays the scheduled date, start and end times, and either the physical address (Offline) or the Google Meet link (Online). Tap the address to open it in your default maps app, or tap the link to launch the meeting directly.
- **Private Notes** — Your personal, non-shared notes attached to this meeting. Only you can see these.
- **Status Badge** — A color-coded label indicating the current state of the meeting (see the table below).
- **Action Buttons** — Context-sensitive buttons at the bottom of the screen based on your role and the meeting's status.

## Meeting statuses

| Status | Color | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Pending** | Yellow | The invitation has been sent but the invitee hasn't yet responded. |
| **Accepted** | Green | All participants have accepted; the meeting is scheduled. |
| **Declined** | Red | The invitee declined the invitation. The meeting won't take place unless rescheduled. |
| **Cancelled** | Gray | The organizer cancelled the meeting after it was accepted or while it was pending. |
| **Completed** | Blue | The meeting has been marked done. |
| **Rescheduled** | Purple | The original time was changed; a new meeting entry is linked to this one. |

## Action buttons

The buttons available to you depend on whether you created the meeting or were invited to it.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="If you're the Organizer">
    - **Edit** — Update the date, time, location, or meeting mode. Saving changes automatically notifies all participants.
    - **Cancel Meeting** — Cancel the meeting and send a cancellation notice to all participants.
    - **Mark Complete** — Mark the meeting as done once it's happened. If this meeting was part of a contact's follow-up cadence, marking it complete automatically advances them to the next stage.
    - **Add / Edit Notes** — Open a full-screen editor to write or update your private notes.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="If you're an Invitee">
    - **Accept** — Accept the meeting invitation. The organizer is notified immediately and the status changes to **Accepted**.
    - **Decline** — Decline the invitation. You'll be prompted to add an optional message explaining why, which is sent to the organizer.
    - **Add / Edit Notes** — Add your own private notes to the meeting record, visible only to you.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Accept or decline a meeting invitation

When someone invites you to a meeting, you get a **push notification** and a banner at the top of your **Meetings** screen. You can respond directly from the notification or from the Meeting Details screen.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invitation">
    Tap the notification banner or navigate to **Meetings** and tap the meeting marked **Pending** to open its detail screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the details">
    Read through the proposed date, time, and location or Meet link before responding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Respond">
    Tap **Accept** or **Decline**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Every meeting action — scheduling, rescheduling, accepting, or declining — sends a push notification to everyone involved. If the meeting has a Google Meet link, rescheduling also sends an email update automatically.
</Note>
