Your meeting history is a running log of every meeting you've scheduled, completed, cancelled, or declined inside MyEventJar — both **One-on-One** and **Network** meetings.

## Access your meeting history

Tap **Meetings** in the bottom navigation bar, then tap the **History** sub-tab. Meetings appear in reverse chronological order, with the most recent at the top.

Each entry shows:
- The contact's name and profile photo
- The meeting date
- The meeting mode (Offline or Online)
- The current status (see [Meeting Details](/meetings/meeting-details) for the full status list)

Tap any entry to open its full Meeting Details screen.
