MyEventJar has two kinds of meetings: **One-on-One** meetings, which live on a contact's follow-up cadence, and **Network** meetings, which you schedule directly with a **Qualified Lead**. Both use a **Google Meet** link when set to Online, sync to your **Google Calendar**, and notify everyone by push notification.

## One-on-One Meetings

One-on-one meetings are how you complete the **7-Day** and **30-Day** stages of a contact's follow-up cadence. Rather than starting from a general "new meeting" screen, they're triggered directly from the contact itself.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contact">
    Expand the contact's card and, once they've reached the **7-Day** or **30-Day** stage, tap the center of their follow-up stage wheel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the meeting mode">
    Select **Offline** or **Online**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a date and time">
    Choose a date, then a time slot. If the contact is a MyEventJar user, the slots shown come from their **Meeting Preferences**, converted into your timezone automatically. If they're not on MyEventJar, your own Meeting Preferences are used instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule">
    Tap **Schedule Meeting**. If you set Online and have Google Calendar connected, a Google Meet link is generated automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once scheduled, the meeting appears on the contact card for both of you (if they're a MyEventJar user), and either side can **reschedule** it or **mark it complete**. Marking it complete automatically advances the contact to the next stage. See [Follow-ups & Insights](/contacts/follow-ups-insights) for the full cadence.

## Network Meetings

Once a contact is a **Qualified Lead**, meetings with them move off the stage wheel and become **Network Meetings** — scheduled from the Meetings tab like any other meeting.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Meetings tab">
    Tap **Meetings** in the bottom navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the + button">
    Tap the **\+** button to start a new meeting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a Qualified Lead">
    Choose the qualified lead you want to meet with from the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose mode, date, and time">
    Select **Offline** or **Online**, then a date and time slot — again pulled from the other person's Meeting Preferences and converted into your timezone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule">
    Tap **Schedule Meeting**. The meeting shows up for both of you under the right date in the **Meetings** tab, and either side can **Accept** or **Complete** it from there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Notifications

Every stage update and meeting action — scheduling, rescheduling, accepting, or declining — sends a **push notification**. If the meeting has a Google Meet link (Google Calendar connected), rescheduling also sends an **email** update automatically.

## See Each Other's Profile

From a meeting, you can open the other person's **Digital Business Card** to see their full profile — and from their Digital Business Card, you can jump straight to their saved contact record.
