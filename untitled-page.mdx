**Meeting Preferences** is the one place that controls how anyone can book time with you — your timezone, your open hours, how much notice you need, and how far ahead people can look. Set it once and every meeting, whether it's a one-on-one follow-up or a network meeting, is built around it automatically.

## Open Meeting Preferences

Go to the **Meetings** tab and tap **Meeting Preferences** in the app bar.

<Tip>
  Tap the banner at the top of the Meeting Preferences screen to connect your **Google Calendar**. Once connected, any time you're already busy is automatically excluded from the slots people see — no one can book over a meeting you already have.
</Tip>

## Available Settings

| Setting | What It Controls |
| --- | --- |
| **Timezone** | The timezone your availability is calculated in. |
| **Slot Interval** | How your open hours are broken into bookable slots (e.g., every 15 or 30 minutes). |
| **Minimum Notice Time** | How much advance notice is required before a slot can be booked. |
| **Buffer Time Before / After** | Padding automatically blocked around every meeting so slots can't be booked back-to-back. |
| **Maximum Advance Booking Days** | The furthest into the future someone can book a meeting with you. |
| **Allowed Meeting Durations** | Which meeting lengths you're willing to accept. |
| **Weekly Availability** | Your open hours for each day, Monday through Sunday. |

## Set Your Weekly Availability

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn days on or off">
    For each day, Monday through Sunday, toggle it on if you're generally open to meetings, or off if you never take meetings that day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your hours for each active day">
    For every day you've turned on, set the time range you're open.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Tap **Save**. Your new availability applies to every future meeting request immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Block Off Specific Dates

Use overrides for anything your weekly schedule doesn't already cover:

- **Import Public Holidays** — Choose a country and MyEventJar marks its public holidays as unavailable automatically.
- **Manually Mark Dates Unavailable** — Pick a specific date, or a specific date and time range, and mark it unavailable — useful for one-off conflicts your weekly schedule wouldn't otherwise catch.

<Note>
  If the person you're scheduling with is also a MyEventJar user, your Meeting Preferences and theirs are matched automatically — no one needs to compare calendars manually, and every time shown is converted into each person's own timezone. If they're not a MyEventJar user, your own preferences are used instead.
</Note>
