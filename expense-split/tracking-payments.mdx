## Your Friends List

Open **Expense Split → Friends** to see everyone in your quick-pick roster, along with any pending invites you've sent or received. Its main purpose is speed — it's the list you pick from when adding people to a new trip, so you're not searching your full contact list every time.

Tap **\+ Add Friend** (floating button) to add someone one of three ways:

- **New Friend** — enter their details manually
- **From My Contacts** — pick from your MyEventJar contacts
- **From Phone Contacts** — pick from your device's contacts

You can also send an invitation by SMS or email. If you've configured **SMTP** under your Profile, the email invite goes out automatically.

Each friend or pending invite has a **⋯** menu:

- **Remove Friend** — for existing friends
- **Resend** or **Cancel** — for invitations you sent
- **Accept** or **Decline** — for invitations you received

<Warning>
  You can't remove a friend while you have an unsettled balance with them — settle up first. Once removed, all transaction history with that friend is deleted for everyone involved, not just you.
</Warning>

To remove a friend you added, long-press their record on the Friends list and swipe left.

## Settling Up

From either the **Expenses** or **Friends** tab inside a trip, tap **Settle Up** at the bottom to open the settle-up view for that trip. At the top you'll see your overall **Spend**, what **You Owe**, and what **You Receive**. Below that, each friend in the trip shows their photo, name, number, what you owe them, what they owe you, and a **Settle Up** button.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Settle Up on a friend">
    A popup shows the amount and what it's for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a payment method">
    Pick **Cash**, **UPI**, **Bank Transfer**, or **Other**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Tap to settle. The balance updates immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also **mark a payment you've received** from a friend the same way, using **Record Payment** instead of Settle Up.

## Transactions

Open **Expense Split → Transactions** to see a running ledger of every settled payment. Scroll the horizontal date selector to a specific month, and you'll see everything you sent and received that month.
