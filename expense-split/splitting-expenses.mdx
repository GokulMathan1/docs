You'll find **Expense Split** under the **More** tab. It's built around three sections: **Trips**, **Friends**, and **Transactions** — group your shared costs by trip, keep a quick-pick roster of people you split with, and see a running ledger of every payment.

You can add both MyEventJar users and people who aren't on MyEventJar yet as trip members or friends. If someone who isn't a MyEventJar user later signs up using the same email you originally added them with, the trip — along with its expenses and members — automatically appears in their account.

## The Trips List

Open **Expense Split → Trips** to see every trip you're part of, sorted by whichever had the most recent activity. Your most recently updated trip is shown **expanded** by default; every other trip appears collapsed, showing just its name, destination, and status.

Tap **\+ New Trip** (floating button) to start one.

Your expanded trip shows:

- Number of expenses and members
- How much you individually owe, and how much you're owed
- The trip's total spend, and whether it's gone over its **daily expense limit**

<Note>
  Only the trip's creator can delete it. Other members can view the trip but don't see a delete option.
</Note>

## Adding Members to a Trip

You can add both MyEventJar users and people who aren't. When adding someone to a trip, you can pick them from your existing **Friends** list, or add them as a brand-new friend — either way, they're added to the trip and, if new, to your Friends list at the same time.

## Inside a Trip

Tap any trip to open it. It has two tabs:

- **Expenses** — every expense logged on this trip
- **Friends** — everyone currently a member of this trip

### Expenses Tab

Expenses are sorted by when they were created, **oldest first** — the most recent expense is always at the bottom.

Each expense shows its name, amount, who paid, and your individual share. Expenses you created appear **right-aligned**, like your own messages in a chat; expenses other members created appear **left-aligned** — so it's easy to scan who paid for what at a glance. If you paid for an expense, it's marked **Paid** along with the amount.

<Tip>
  Long-press and swipe left on an expense you created to **close** it.
</Tip>

### Add an Expense

Tap **\+ Add Expense** (floating button) from the Expenses tab. Fill in:

- **Title**
- **Amount**
- **Category**
- **Paid By**
- **Members Involved**
- **Description** (optional)

Then tap **Create**.

<Warning>
  Once an expense is created, you **can't change the amount, who paid, or who's involved**. The only edits available to the creator afterward are renaming the expense or closing it entirely.
</Warning>

## Share a Trip by QR or Link

Tap the **QR icon** in the top-right of a trip's app bar to show a QR code and a shareable link. Tap **Share** to send it by email, WhatsApp, or your device's share sheet. Whoever opens the link is taken into MyEventJar, goes through verification, and is automatically added as a member of the trip.
