## Overview

MyEventJar can automatically generate a Zoom meeting link whenever a meeting is scheduled with you, so you never have to create one manually. This guide walks through connecting your Zoom account from your MyEventJar profile.

<Note>
  You'll need an active Zoom account to complete this setup. MyEventJar requests permission to view and create meetings on your behalf — it never accesses your recordings or chat history.
</Note>

## Steps to connect Zoom

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to MyEventJar">
    Go to [MyEventJar.com](https://myeventjar.com) and sign in with your email and password, or use one of the quick sign-in options (Google, LinkedIn, or WhatsApp).

    <Frame>
      ![Screenshot Localhost 8080 2026 09 10 18 32 16](/images/screenshot-localhost_8080-2026.09.10-18_32_16.png "Screenshot Localhost 8080 2026 09 10 18 32 16")
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    Once signed in, navigate to **My Profile**. Scroll down to the **Integrations** card — this lists all connected services, including Email, WhatsApp, Google Calendar, and Zoom.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Integrations panel">
    Click the edit icon on the **Integrations** card, or select the **Zoom** row directly. This opens the Integrations panel on the right side of the screen.

    <Frame>
      ![Screenshot Localhost 8080 2026 09 10 18 32 41](/images/screenshot-localhost_8080-2026.09.10-18_32_41.png "Screenshot Localhost 8080 2026 09 10 18 32 41")
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Zoom tab">
    In the Integrations panel, switch to the **Zoom** tab. You'll see a short description: _"Create Zoom meeting links automatically for your meetings."_
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect Zoom">
    Click the **Connect Zoom** button.

    <Frame>
      ![Screenshot Localhost 8080 2026 09 10 18 33 30](/images/screenshot-localhost_8080-2026.09.10-18_33_30.png "Screenshot Localhost 8080 2026 09 10 18 33 30")
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to your Zoom account">
    A Zoom sign-in window opens. Enter your Zoom **email address** and **password**, then click **Sign In**. If you're already signed in to Zoom in your browser, this step is skipped automatically.

    <Tip>
      You can also sign in with SSO, Apple, Google, Facebook, or Microsoft if your Zoom account uses one of those methods.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize MyEventJar">
    Zoom will show an authorization screen listing the permissions MyEventJar is requesting:

    - View a meeting
    - Create a meeting for a user
    - View a user

    Review the permissions, then click **Allow** to continue. Click **Decline** if you'd rather not proceed.

    <Frame>
      ![Screen 3](/images/screen-3.png "Screen 3")
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Login into your Zoom Account">
    Zoom will show an Login screen and then enter your account info and then connect a Zoom into MyEventJar.

    <Frame>
      ![Screen2](/images/screen2.png "Screen2")
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the connection">
    You'll be redirected back to MyEventJar. The Zoom tab now shows a green **Connected** status along with the Zoom account email that's linked. The Integrations list on your profile will also update to show Zoom as **Connected**.

    <Frame>
      ![Screen](/images/screen.png "Screen")
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Disconnecting Zoom

If you ever need to unlink your Zoom account, open the same **Integrations → Zoom** tab and click **Disconnect**. Future meetings will no longer generate Zoom links automatically until you reconnect.

<Warning>
  Disconnecting Zoom does not cancel or delete meeting links that were already created — it only stops new links from being generated.
</Warning>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The Zoom sign-in window doesn't appear">
    Make sure pop-ups are allowed for myeventjar.com in your browser settings, then click **Connect Zoom** again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I clicked Allow but the status still shows Not connected">
    Refresh the profile page. If the issue persists, disconnect (if partially connected) and repeat the connection steps.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I connect a different Zoom account later?">
    Yes — disconnect the current account first, then click **Connect Zoom** and sign in with the new account when prompted.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
