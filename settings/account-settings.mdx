Everything on this page lives under your **Profile** — open it and scroll down to find each section below.

## Edit Your Profile

Everything that appears on your Digital Business Card — your name, photo, professional info, networking preferences, and personal touches — is set from **Profile → Edit**. See [Digital Business Card](/digital-business-card) for the full list of fields and what each one does, plus how to preview and share your card once it's filled in.

## Integrations (Automation)

Scroll down on your Profile to **Automation**. It opens to two tabs: **Email** and **WhatsApp**.

### Email

Connect one of five providers:

- **Gmail** — connect via OAuth, or manually with an app password
- **Outlook** — same as Gmail: OAuth or app password
- **SendGrid**
- **Mailgun**
- **Custom SMTP server**

### WhatsApp

Connect using a **MyBotify token**, generated at [mybotify.com](https://whatsapp.mybotify.com/).

<Note>
  These are the same Email and WhatsApp channels used to send follow-up messages and meeting invites automatically — see [Follow-ups & Insights](/contacts/follow-ups-insights) and [Scheduling Meetings](/meetings/scheduling-meetings).
</Note>

## Google Calendar

Google Calendar is connected from the banner on the **Meeting Preferences** screen, not from here. See [Meeting Preferences](/meeting-preferences/overview) for how it works and what it unlocks.

## App Permissions

MyEventJar asks for two device permissions: **Camera** and **Notifications** (push). Handle both from the same place:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open App Permissions">
    On your Profile, scroll down to **App Permissions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle Camera access">
    Turn **Camera** on to scan QR codes and visiting cards from within the app, or off to disable camera access entirely.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle Notification access">
    Turn **Notifications** on to receive push notifications, or off to stop them. See your Notifications settings page for everything push notifications cover.
  </Step>
</Steps>

There's no further customization beyond each on/off toggle.

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open 2FA setup">
    Go to **Two-Factor Authentication** and start setup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your authenticator app">
    Use an authenticator app — **Google Authenticator** or **Microsoft Authenticator** both work — and either scan the QR code shown on screen, or copy the setup token and paste it in manually.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the code">
    Enter the 6-digit code your authenticator app generates to finish setup.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once enabled, **every login** — not just new devices — prompts you for your 6-digit code after your password.

## Change Your Password

Tap **Change Password** and confirm the email on your account. If it matches what's on file, MyEventJar sends a reset link to that address — open it to set your new password.

## Active Sessions

Go to **Active Sessions** to see every device currently signed in, and sign out of any you don't recognize or no longer use.

## Deactivate Your Account

Tap **Deactivate Account** and enter your password to confirm. Deactivating isn't immediate deletion — you get a **30-day** grace period:

- **Reactivate within 30 days** and your account, contacts, and data are restored exactly as they were.
- **Don't reactivate**, and your account and all its data are **permanently deleted** once the 30 days are up.
