MyEventJar keeps you updated with push notifications so you don't have to keep checking the app. There's a single notification permission — no per-type toggles, custom timing, or quiet hours to configure — and turning it on covers everything below.

## Turn On Notifications

Go to **Profile → Notifications** and enable push notifications. See [App Navigation](/getting-started/app-navigation) for where this sits under Profile & Settings.

## What You'll Be Notified About

### Contacts & Follow-ups

- A contact's **stage** moves forward (New → 24h → 7d → 30d → Qualified)
- A contact's follow-up is **overdue**
- A reminder to **add contacts**

See [Follow-ups & Insights](/contacts/follow-ups-insights) for how the stage cadence works.

### Meetings

For both **one-on-one** and **network** meetings:

- Scheduled
- Rescheduled
- Accepted
- Completed

See [Scheduling Meetings](/meetings/scheduling-meetings).

### Connections

From the Event Info section, where you can send a connection request to an attendee:

- Connection **received**
- Connection **accepted**
- Connection **rejected**

See [Adding Contacts](/contacts/adding-contacts) for how event connections work.

### Your NIS Score

A daily notification with your current **Network Intelligence Score**. See [Networking Scorecard](/networking-scorecard/overview).

### Events & Tickets

- Ticket **confirmation** after purchase
- Event **updated**
- Event **cancelled**
- Event reminders, sent **1 hour** and **2 hours** before the event starts

### Integrations

- Status updates for your **email** and **WhatsApp** integrations

### Profile

- A nudge if your **profile is incomplete**
