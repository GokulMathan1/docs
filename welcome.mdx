## What You Can Do with MyEventJar

<Columns cols={2}>
  <div>
  MyEventJar brings together the tools you need to **network** with purpose:

  * **Digital Business Card** — Create a personalized card with your photo, role, and links. Share it via QR code or link in seconds.
  * **Contacts & Networking** — Add **contacts** your way: scan a **Business or Visiting Card**, tap an **NFC Card**, scan a **QR Code**, or use **Manual entry**. Attend business events to meet new people, then convert each connection into a saved **contact** and follow up with them stage by stage.
  * **Meetings** — Schedule one-on-one meetings, set your **meeting preferences**, and connect your **Google Calendar** to automatically block out busy slots so no one can double-book you.
  * **Expense Split** — Log shared expenses from events or trips and track who owes what.
  * **[Networking Intelligence Scorecard (NIS)](https://docs.myeventjar.com/networking-scorecard/overview)** — Get an **Intelligence Scorecard** that visualizes how active and effective your networking has been, so you can track and improve engagement over time.

  <Tip>
    New to MyEventJar? Start with the [Getting Started guide](/getting-started/overview) to set up your account and profile.
  </Tip>
</div>
