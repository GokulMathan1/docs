Whether you're just getting started with MyEventJar or you've been using it for a while, you'll find answers to the most common questions right here. Browse the topics below, and if you still can't find what you're looking for, reach out to our support team.

---

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="Is MyEventJar free to use?">
    Networking is free — contacts, meetings, your Network Intelligence Score, and Expense Split don't require a paid plan.

    **Publishing events as an organizer** is the one part that requires an active subscription, set up on the web dashboard at [myeventjar.com](https://myeventjar.com). See [Subscription](/settings/subscription) for plan details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I recover my password?">
    1. On the login screen, tap **Forgot Password?**
    2. Enter the email address on your account and tap **Send Reset Link**.
    3. Open the email from MyEventJar and tap the reset link.
    4. Set your new password.

    You can also change your password anytime while signed in — see [Account Settings](/settings/account-settings).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I lose my phone?">
    Your account lives in the cloud, so losing your device doesn't mean losing your data:

    1. Sign in on your new device with your email and password, your phone number (if you use Phone + WhatsApp OTP to log in), or a linked social account.
    2. If 2FA is enabled, enter the code from your authenticator app.
    3. Go to **Account Settings → Active Sessions** and sign out your lost device.
    4. Consider changing your password as an extra precaution.

    If you can't sign in at all, contact [support@myeventjar.com](mailto:support@myeventjar.com).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my data private and secure?">
    MyEventJar doesn't sell your personal data to advertisers or third parties. You can add an extra layer of protection to your account with **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)** — see [Account Settings](/settings/account-settings) for setup.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I delete my account?">
    You don't delete your account outright — you **deactivate** it:

    1. Go to **Account Settings → Deactivate Account**.
    2. Enter your password to confirm.

    Deactivating starts a **30-day grace period**. Sign back in within 30 days and everything is restored exactly as it was. If you don't reactivate within that window, your account and all its data are permanently deleted. See [Account Settings](/settings/account-settings).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use MyEventJar on a desktop computer?">
    The **app is the preferred way** to use MyEventJar — it's built as **iOS** and **Android** apps for day-to-day networking. That said, you can also sign in on the **web** at [myeventjar.com](https://myeventjar.com) and use the same features there. Event organizers specifically use the web dashboard to create and manage events and their subscription.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I invite someone to MyEventJar?">
    Share your **Digital Business Card** — tap the **Share** icon on your Profile to send your QR code or personalized link by email, WhatsApp, or any other app. Anyone who opens it can save your details instantly, whether or not they're already a MyEventJar user. See [Digital Business Card](/digital-business-card/overview).

    Looking to add someone specifically as a **Friend** for splitting expenses? See [Friends & Settling Up](/expense-split/friends-and-settling-up).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does MyEventJar work offline?">
    No — MyEventJar needs an active internet connection for all features, including scanning contacts and QR codes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I report a problem or bug?">
    The most direct way to reach us is by emailing [support@myeventjar.com](mailto:support@myeventjar.com) with a description of what happened.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
