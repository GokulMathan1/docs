Running into a hiccup with MyEventJar? Most issues can be resolved quickly with a few targeted steps. Work through the relevant section below. If none of the steps resolve your issue, reach out to our support team using the contact information at the bottom of this page.

---

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="The app won't open or keeps crashing">
    If MyEventJar closes immediately after opening, or freezes during the loading screen, try the following steps in order:

    1. **Force-quit the app** — on iOS, swipe up from the bottom and swipe the MyEventJar card away. On Android, open Recent Apps and swipe it off. Then relaunch.
    2. **Restart your device** — a full reboot clears temporary system issues that can block apps from launching.
    3. **Check for updates** — open the App Store or Google Play, search for MyEventJar, and install any available update.
    4. **Check available storage** — free up space if your device is nearly full.
    5. **Reinstall the app** — your data is stored in the cloud and syncs back after you sign in.

    If it still crashes after reinstalling, email support with your device model and OS version so we can investigate.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't log in to my account">
    1. **Double-check your credentials** — confirm you're using the right email address or phone number.
    2. **Reset your password** — tap **Forgot Password?** on the login screen and follow the reset link sent to your inbox. Check spam if it doesn't arrive.
    3. **Check your 2FA code** — make sure your device's clock is accurate, since authenticator codes are time-based.
    4. **Try your original sign-in method** — if you signed up with **Phone + WhatsApp OTP**, **Google**, **LinkedIn**, or **Apple**, use that option instead of email and password.
    5. **Clear the app cache** (Android: **Settings → Apps → MyEventJar → Storage → Clear Cache**) or **reinstall** (iOS) — your cloud data is unaffected either way.

    Still locked out? Contact [support@myeventjar.com](mailto:support@myeventjar.com) for account recovery.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My QR code won't scan (or I can't scan someone else's)">
    **If someone can't scan your QR code:**
    1. Increase your screen brightness to maximum before showing your code.
    2. Make sure the code has fully loaded on screen.
    3. Ask the other person to tap to focus their camera on your code.
    4. As an alternative, share your profile via **Share → Copy Link** instead.

    **If you can't scan someone else's:**
    1. Confirm MyEventJar has camera access — go to **Account Settings → App Permissions** and make sure **Camera** is enabled.
    2. Clean your camera lens.
    3. Hold your phone steady, about 15–25 cm from the code.
    4. Try better lighting — avoid direct glare or a very dark room.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My meetings aren't syncing with Google Calendar">
    1. **Check your connection** — open **Meeting Preferences** and confirm the banner shows Google Calendar as connected. If not, tap it to connect.
    2. **Reconnect** — if it looks connected but isn't syncing, disconnect and reconnect from the same banner.
    3. **Confirm the account** — make sure you're signed in to the same Google account in MyEventJar and in your device's Calendar app.
    4. **Give it a minute** — sync isn't always instant.

    Google Calendar is currently the only calendar MyEventJar connects to.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I'm not receiving push notifications">
    1. **Check the OS-level permission** — on iOS, **Settings → Notifications → MyEventJar → Allow Notifications**; on Android, **Settings → Apps → MyEventJar → Notifications**.
    2. **Check the in-app toggle** — go to **Account Settings → App Permissions** and make sure **Notifications** is on.
    3. **Check Do Not Disturb / Focus mode** isn't blocking MyEventJar.
    4. **Toggle it off and back on**, both at the OS level and in the app.
    5. **Reinstall** as a last resort — your data is stored in the cloud.

    There's no per-category notification toggle inside MyEventJar — it's a single on/off switch. See your Notifications page for everything push notifications cover.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="An expense isn't showing up or updating correctly">
    1. **Check your connection** — creating and settling expenses needs an active connection.
    2. **Confirm the trip** — every expense lives inside a **Trip**, not a standalone list. Make sure you're looking in the right trip's Expenses tab.
    3. **Confirm the other person is a Friend** — if they're not a MyEventJar user yet, they're invited by SMS or email instead of getting an in-app notification.
    4. **Refresh** — pull down on the trip's Expenses tab.
    5. **Remember expenses lock after creation** — the amount, payer, and members involved can't be changed once an expense is created. The creator can only rename it or close it. See [Trips & Expenses](/expense-split/trips-and-expenses).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My Digital Business Card link isn't working">
    1. **Check your Profile is filled in** — your card pulls directly from your Profile fields. If it looks empty, add at least your name under **Profile → Edit**. See [Digital Business Card](/digital-business-card/overview).
    2. **Share a fresh link** — tap **Share** on your card and send the current QR code or link.
    3. **Try a different browser** — ask the recipient to try another browser or an incognito/private window if the link opens but looks broken.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't find an event I'm looking for">
    1. **Check where you're looking** — Events live under **More → Events & Tickets**, not a dedicated bottom-nav tab.
    2. **Check My Tickets** — events you already attended move to the **Completed** tab under **My Tickets**.
    3. **Clear your filters** — Category, Date, Online/Offline, and Paid/Free filters on the browse screen can hide an event that doesn't match.
    4. **Search by name** — use the **Search** icon on the Events screen.
    5. **Ask the organizer** — if you were invited privately, they may have cancelled or unlisted it.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
