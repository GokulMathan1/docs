Your MyEventJar Digital Business Card is a living, always-current profile you carry in your pocket. It doubles as your public **Bio Page** — a single link you can share on **LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram, X, Facebook**, or anywhere else. Anyone who opens it can view your details, save you to their contacts, and **book a one-on-one meeting** with you in a single tap.

## Why Go Digital?

Paper business cards have served professionals for decades, but they come with real limitations. They run out, they become outdated the moment your phone number changes, and they're easy to misplace. Your MyEventJar Digital Business Card solves all of that.

## What Your Card Displays

Everything your card can show is set in one place — your **Profile**. Open **Profile → Edit**, fill in as much as you'd like, and tap the **eye icon** at the top of the screen anytime to preview exactly how your Digital Business Card looks to others.

### Professional Info

| Field | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Professional Title** | Your current role or professional identity |
| **Business Name** | Your organization or, if independent, your own brand name |
| **Business Category** | The industry or category your work falls under |
| **Business Email & Phone** | Tap-to-email and tap-to-call contact details |
| **Business Address** | Where you're based |
| **Operating Region(s)** | The regions you work across |
| **Business Locations** | Any additional locations you operate from |
| **Website** | A direct link to your site, portfolio, or booking page |
| **Professional Summary** | A short write-up of your background and experience, in your own words |

### Networking Preferences

| Field | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **What You're Looking For** | What you want to get out of networking right now |
| **Who You're Open to Connecting With** | The kinds of people or roles you'd like to meet |
| **How You Can Help Others** | What you bring to the table for your connections |
| **Topics You're Open to Discussing** | Subjects you're happy to talk shop about |
| **Networking Goal** | Your personal networking target, tracked alongside your [Networking Intelligence Score](/networking-scorecard/overview) |

### Personal Touches

| Field | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Known Languages** | Languages you're comfortable networking in |
| **Skills** | Skills you want to be known for |
| **Gallery** | A few photos that add personality beyond your profile picture |
| **Social & Contact Links** | LinkedIn, X (Twitter), Instagram, GitHub, and more |

### Activity & Recognition

These show up automatically on your card — there's nothing to fill in:

| Field | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Events Attended** | A live count of the events you've taken part in |
| **Total Contacts** | A live count of the people in your network |
| **Badges Earned** | Recognition awarded by event organizers |

<Tip>
  Badges are usually assigned by an event's organizer once you've attended one of their events. Holding the right badge can matter beyond bragging rights — some organizers restrict entry to a future event to attendees who already hold a specific badge from them.
</Tip>

<Note>
  You choose exactly which fields appear on your card. Leave anything blank and it simply won't show — only the fields you fill in are visible to recipients.
</Note>

## How Recipients Save Your Card

When someone receives your card — whether via QR code, shared link, NFC tap, or an in-app send — they see a clean profile page with one clear call to action. They can:

- **Save to Contacts** — exports a standard `.vcf` contact file that drops straight into their phone's address book.
- **Add as a Contact in MyEventJar** — if they're already a MyEventJar user, saving you creates a two-way synced contact on both sides, and starts the same follow-up cadence covered in [Managing Contacts](/contacts/managing-contacts) and [Follow-ups & Insights](/contacts/follow-ups-insights).
- **Copy individual details** — tap any field to copy a phone number, email address, or link without saving the whole card.

No app download is required for recipients who receive a shared link — your card works in any modern mobile or desktop browser.

## Set Up and Share Your Card

There's no separate setup flow — everything above lives in the same place.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fill in your Profile">
    Go to **Profile → Edit** and add as much as you'd like across your professional info, networking preferences, and personal touches. Every field is optional except your name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview your card">
    Tap the **eye icon** at the top of the Profile screen anytime to see exactly how your card will look to the people you share it with.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it">
    Tap the **Share** icon, or send your **QR code** or your **personalized link** — whichever's easiest in the moment.
  </Step>
</Steps>
